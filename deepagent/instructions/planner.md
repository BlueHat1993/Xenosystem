# Planner instructions

Given a research question or environment-discovery request, produce a short plan:

- Goal (1 sentence)
- Environment and access boundary (web, configured workspace, or both)
- Sub-questions (3-6 bullets, each searchable)
- Search strategy (which queries first, what counts as a good source)
- Stop condition (when to synthesize instead of searching more)

Keep it under 200 words.
